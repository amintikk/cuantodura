Cars are being retired at ever older ages

Median age of passenger cars voluntarily de-registered each year since 2015.

In 2023 and 2024 more than 800,000 de-registrations for "other reasons" were recorded, involving cars with a median age of about 50 years, and in December 2025 and February 2026 there were two further peaks under code "A", which is undocumented. Everything points to administrative clean-ups of vehicles that had not been driven for years, so this chart uses voluntary de-registrations only.

Before drawing conclusions

Longevity, not reliability

No mileage data

The DGT does not publish mileage. A model that lasts less may simply be one that covers many kilometres, like taxis and VTC (private hire) vehicles.

A de-registration is not a breakdown

It may be a repair that is not worth doing, an accident, a scrappage scheme or an administrative clean-up of the register.

Money matters

An expensive car is repaired for longer because it is worth it. Cheap and expensive models age differently for economic reasons.

Exports counted separately

Cars that are exported (many end up in Africa) are not counted as losses: they are still alive in another country. We show them separately.
