Purpose

Cuánto dura is a personal, free, non-profit and open-source project that calculates the longevity of car models in Spain from public DGT data. No products or services are sold, there is no advertising and no registration is required.

Nature of the information

The figures are statistical estimates for the set of cars of each model and describe their longevity on the register, not their mechanical reliability. They are not an assessment of any specific vehicle, nor advice on buying, repairing or insuring one. The methodology and its limits are described on the methodology page.

Source of the data

Data source: Dirección General de Tráfico (DGT). The DGT does not participate in, sponsor or endorse this project. Details and reuse conditions are in sources.
