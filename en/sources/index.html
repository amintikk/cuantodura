Reuse conditions

We reuse public sector information under Ley 37/2007 (Spain's public sector information re-use law) and the general reuse conditions for the Spanish Government's open data: we cite the source and the update date, we do not alter the meaning of the information, and we do not state or suggest that the DGT participates in, sponsors or endorses this project. The figures on this website are our own work based on that data; any calculation or interpretation errors are ours, not the DGT's.

The microdata are published anonymised: the DGT masks infrequent model values and does not include owner data. We do not store or publish individual records, only aggregates.

Download the aggregated data

Everything you see on the website is served as JSON. You may reuse it, citing the DGT as the source and this website for the processing:

  • overview.json: national table, fuel types, segments and history since 2015.
  • index.json: summary of all models.
  • provinces.json: provinces.
  • rankings.json: brands.
  • /data/m/<model>.json: full record for each model, for example seat-ibiza.json.

The code that downloads the DGT files and calculates the tables is on GitHub under the MIT licence.
