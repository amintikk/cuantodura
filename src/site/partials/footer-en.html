Cuánto dura

How long cars really last in Spain, computed from the public de-registration and fleet microdata of the DGT (Spain's traffic authority). A free, open-source personal project.

Data source: Dirección General de Tráfico (DGT), MATRABA and monthly fleet microdata. Data last updated: {{data-updated-en}}. The DGT does not take part in or sponsor this project.
